### Beneficiary Selection Criteria

1. **Age:** 16-35 years
2. **Residency:** LGA resident
3. **Motivation:** Demonstrated interest
4. **Priority Groups:**
   - Women (minimum 50%)
   - Persons with disabilities
   - Unemployed youth
   - Vulnerable groups
