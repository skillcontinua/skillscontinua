# SkillsContinua Pilot Program Proposal
## Presented to His Excellency Governor Alex Otti, OFR
## Abia State Government

---

## Executive Summary

SkillsContinua is a comprehensive digital skills and vocational training platform designed to take individuals from where they are to where they want to be. This proposal seeks partnership with the Abia State Government to launch a pilot program that will equip 500 Abia youths with marketable skills, with the primary objective of creating self-employed, independent individuals who can generate their own income and contribute to the economy.

---

## The Problem

Abia State, like many states in Nigeria, faces significant challenges:

- **Youth Unemployment:** High rates of unemployment among young people
- **Skills Gap:** Mismatch between available skills and market demands
- **Economic Dependence:** Over-reliance on government jobs
- **Underemployment:** Many working in low-income, informal sector jobs

---

## The Solution: SkillsContinua

SkillsContinua is a platform that offers:

- **306 Courses** across 39 categories
- **4,114 Lessons** with practical exercises
- **6 Languages** (English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Swahili, Arabic)
- **4 Learning Approaches** (Pedagogic, Andragogic, Heutagogic, Cybergogic)
- **Self-Employment Focus** - Creating job creators, not job seekers

---

## Pilot Program Structure

### Phase 1: Beneficiary Selection (Month 1)
- **500 beneficiaries** across 2-3 LGAs
- Youth (16-35 years old)
- Women and persons with disabilities prioritized
- Merit-based, transparent selection

### Phase 2: Learning Journey (Months 2-6)
| Week | Activity | Outcome |
|------|----------|---------|
| 1-2 | Orientation & Course Selection | Personalized learning path |
| 3-14 | Self-paced online/offline learning | Skill acquisition |
| 15-16 | Practical workshops & mentorship | Applied knowledge |
| 17-20 | Assessment & Certification | Recognized qualification |

### Phase 3: Self-Employment Support (Months 6-9)
- Business plan development
- Mentorship from successful entrepreneurs
- Access to markets and networks
- Startup support
