# SkillsContinua Pilot Program
## Presented to His Excellency Governor Alex Otti, OFR
## Abia State Government

---

## Executive Summary

SkillsContinua is a comprehensive digital skills and vocational training platform designed to take individuals from where they are to where they want to be. This proposal seeks partnership with the Abia State Government to launch a pilot program that will equip **500 Abia youths** with marketable skills, with the primary objective of creating **self-employed, independent individuals** who can generate their own income and contribute to the economy.

## Pilot Program Structure

### Beneficiary Selection
- **Target:** 500 beneficiaries
- **Age:** 16-35 years
- **Gender:** 50% women minimum
- **Priority:** Persons with disabilities
- **Locations:** 3 LGAs initially

### Training Journey
| Phase | Duration | Activity |
|-------|----------|----------|
| 1 | Month 1 | Orientation & Course Selection |
| 2 | Months 2-4 | Online & Practical Training |
| 3 | Month 5 | Mentorship & Workshops |
| 4 | Month 6 | Assessment & Certification |

### Self-Employment Support
- Business plan development
- Mentorship from successful entrepreneurs
- Access to markets and networks
- Startup support
