# Governor Meeting Preparation Guide

## Meeting Structure (30-45 minutes)

| Time | Topic | Key Points |
|------|-------|------------|
| 0-5 min | Introduction | Renaissance Stream Foundation, Mission |
| 5-10 min | Problem | Unemployment, Skills Gap |
| 10-15 min | Solution | SkillsContinua Platform |
| 15-20 min | Pilot Program | 500 beneficiaries, Self-employment focus |
| 20-25 min | Impact | Economic growth, Job creation |
| 25-30 min | Call to Action | Partnership, Next steps |

## Key Talking Points

### 1. Problem Statement
- Abia State youth unemployment rate: High
- Many young people lack marketable skills
- Over-reliance on government jobs

### 2. Solution
- 306 courses across 39 categories
- 4,114 lessons with practical exercises
- 6 languages supported
- Self-employment focus

### 3. Impact
- 500 youth trained
- 65% become self-employed
- 191+ new businesses created
- Economic growth for Abia State

### 4. Partnership
- SkillsContinua provides platform and content
- Abia State provides venues and support
- Beneficiary selection and management
- Joint monitoring and evaluation

## Questions to Anticipate

### Q: "What is the cost?"
A: ₦91,000,000 total investment for 500 beneficiaries

### Q: "How do you ensure quality?"
A: Comprehensive curriculum, practical training, certification

### Q: "What happens after training?"
A: Self-employment support, mentorship, business creation

### Q: "Why should we partner?"
A: Economic growth, job creation, youth empowerment, self-reliance
