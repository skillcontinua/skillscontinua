## Learning Center Requirements

### Facility Requirements
- **Space:** Minimum 100 sq meters
- **Classrooms:** 2-3 rooms
- **Electricity:** Reliable power (solar backup recommended)
- **Internet:** Stable internet connection
- **Water:** Clean water supply
- **Accessibility:** Accessible for all (including PWDs)

## Beneficiary Selection

### Criteria
1. **Age:** 16-35 years
2. **Residency:** LGA resident
3. **Education:** Basic literacy preferred
4. **Motivation:** Demonstrated interest in skills training
5. **Disability:** Persons with disabilities prioritized
6. **Gender:** Women prioritized (minimum 50%)

### Selection Process
1. **Announcement:** Public awareness campaign
2. **Application:** Online and physical forms
3. **Screening:** Verification of criteria
4. **Interview:** Assess motivation and commitment
5. **Selection:** Final beneficiary list
6. **Orientation:** Program introduction

## Sustainability Strategy

### Revenue Streams
1. **Government Funding:** State and federal support
2. **Private Sector:** Corporate partnerships
3. **International Donors:** Grants and development funding
4. **Beneficiary Contributions:** Graduates contribute
5. **Social Enterprise:** Income-generating activities

### Self-Sustaining Model
- **Train-the-Trainer:** Graduates become facilitators
- **Business Incubation:** Support for graduate businesses
- **Community Ownership:** Local management and oversight
- **Revenue Generation:** Centers become self-financing
